Hi, I'm Annicka

Curious by nature. Creative on purpose.

I'm an engineering student and creator who notices the tiny things that make a product interesting—the click, the texture, the packaging, the weirdly satisfying detail you immediately want to show somebody else.

My style is bright and playful without feeling overly polished. I like content with movement, personality and a little curiosity built in, so the product feels discovered rather than presented.
